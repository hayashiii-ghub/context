## Overview

The real macOS recording is the visual identity. The composition supports the
footage without adding a second graphic language.

## The Frame

- Full-bleed 16:9 footage on the dark Context canvas.
- Preserve the real menu bar, Finder windows, shelf, Notch Island, and target app.
- The website supplies the outer rounded frame and border; the video does not.

## Composition Rules

- No titles, captions, labels, logo stings, decorative overlays, or fake product UI.
- Crop to 16:9 from the top edge of the recorded Mac display so the menu bar and
  Notch remain visible; remove only the unused lower 263 pixels.
- Keep every camera treatment at or below 1.02x scale with a top-center origin.
- Related beats dissolve in 0.16 seconds after a 0.10-second hidden pre-roll;
  motion remains calm and continuous without doubled UI lingering on screen.
- Never grade the footage away from its recorded neutral-dark appearance.

## Typography

Typography is defined for deterministic fallback only. This composition renders
no text.
